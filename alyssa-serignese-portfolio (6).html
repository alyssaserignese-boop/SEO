BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

Why this actually matters

If AEO still sounds like a buzzword, start here. This is the part that explains why it's worth your time.

The shift

There's no page two anymore

Search used to hand you ten links and let you decide. Now a growing number of people ask ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview one question and get one answer. If your business isn't part of that answer, you're not in second place, you're not in the running at all.

The risk

It's already forming an opinion of you

These tools are answering questions about your business right now, using whatever they can find: an old review, a thin about page, a competitor's comparison article. Doing nothing doesn't keep you neutral. It just means you don't get a say in what gets said.

The opportunity

Most businesses haven't touched this yet

This is roughly where SEO was well over a decade ago: genuinely new, unevenly adopted, with real ground to gain for whoever moves first. The businesses that get their structure right now are the ones these tools keep coming back to later.

Worth saying plainly: this space moves fast. What counts as best practice today can shift within months as models and platforms change, and a lot of what gets sold as an "AI SEO strategy" right now is repackaged guesswork. Part of the job, honestly, is staying close enough to it to tell the difference between what's actually working and what's just a buzzword with a price tag on it.

That's the layer I work in: taking what's already true and good about your business and putting it in a shape the AI tools can actually find, trust, and repeat back.

WAIT, WHAT IS AEO

Pick your level

Same answer, two versions. Pick whichever one you actually want.

You know how you used to just type your question into Google and get a list of websites to click on? A lot of people don't do that anymore. They ask ChatGPT, or they ask Google's AI answer at the top of the page, or they ask Perplexity, and the AI just tells them the answer straight away, sometimes without them clicking on anything at all.

So if the AI doesn't know your business exists, or doesn't trust it enough to mention it, you basically don't exist for that person. AEO is the work of making sure the AI actually knows who you are, gets your facts right, and picks you when it's putting an answer together. Think of it like making sure your business is the one your smartest, most-Googled friend recommends, except the friend is a robot that a lot of people ask now.

AEO (also called generative engine optimisation, or GEO) is the practice of structuring content, metadata, and technical signals so LLM-backed answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) can parse, extract, and cite it with confidence.

In practice that means: FAQPage, Organisation, and Article schema (JSON-LD); direct-answer content blocks written to match natural-language query phrasing rather than short-tail keywords; confirmed crawler access for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, Google-Extended, and PerplexityBot; named author attribution with visible credentials and last-reviewed dates for E-E-A-T signal; and third-party trust signals, reviews, citations, backlink quality, that models weight when deciding whether a source is safe to ground an answer in. It runs alongside, not instead of, traditional technical and content SEO.

METHOD

How I approach an AEO project

This is the framework I use to take a business from invisible to citable across AI search tools. Anonymised from a recent client engagement.

A note on order: each stage feeds data to the next. Skipping ahead to AEO content before the technical foundation and keyword map are in place usually means redoing the work later.
01

Fix the technical foundation

Site health, HTTPS, crawl errors, meta descriptions. Technical issues suppress both traditional rankings and AI crawler trust, regardless of how good the content is.

02

Keyword research and page mapping

One primary keyword per page, clustered by intent, mapped against what competitors already own. This is the layer that tells every other workstream what to actually build.

03

Google Business Profile audit

Categories, service descriptions, and review strategy, informed by the keyword data above. GBP feeds directly into AI Overviews and local AI-assisted results, not just the map pack.

04

Structure content for AI citation

FAQ schema, plain-language definitional content, named author attribution with dates. AI engines weight structured, human-attributed, dated content far more heavily than generic marketing copy.

05

Publish against the map

Content calendar driven entirely by the keyword clusters from step two, sequenced by buyer journey stage so nothing gets published without a clear purpose.

06

Build authority in parallel

Directory listings, relevant industry guest content, and backlink quality review, running alongside publishing rather than after it.
